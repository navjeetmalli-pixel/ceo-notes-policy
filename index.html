The Note Of A CEO Uploader is a personal, single-user application that publishes short videos of original notes on leadership, mindset and work habits, and of sourced quotes from business leaders with original commentary to the owner's own YouTube channel using YouTube API Services. It is not distributed to, or usable by, anyone other than the channel owner.

What it does

  • Uploads finished puzzle videos to the owner's channel (videos.insert)
  • Sets each video's title, description and tags
  • Creates a playlist and adds each video to it
  • Reads back the owner's own channel, video and playlist details to confirm success

What it does not do

  • It never accesses any other user's YouTube data
  • It stores no viewer or subscriber data
  • It does not embed, play or modify the YouTube player
